A little
about me.

I’m Romain. I spent most of my career in hospitality, which taught me to pay attention to detail, presentation and the small touches that make a difference.

Photography has been part of my life for years. What started with travel and street photography gradually became something I wanted to take further.

Today, I’m focused on product and food photography, creating simple, thoughtful images with strong light, texture and detail.
