About

A quiet way of holding time.

Photography, for me, is a way of holding time — fragments of emotion, light, and stillness preserved as memory.

I’m drawn to atmosphere, simplicity, and the subtle tension between presence and absence. My work often begins with a place, but what interests me most is what lingers beneath the surface: mood, silence, texture, and the feeling a moment leaves behind.

Through fine art and travel photography, I try to create images that feel both intimate and timeless — less about documentation, and more about resonance.
