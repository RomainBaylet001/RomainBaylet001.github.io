About

I'm Romain, a French photographer based in Asia.

I used to spend a lot of time thinking about the future. Photography became my way of slowing down, being present, and appreciating what's right in front of me.

I'm drawn to unfamiliar places, the beauty of everyday life, and the small details that often go unnoticed.

Through my photographs, I simply want to share the world as I see it, one moment at a time.
