The Photographer

A little
about me.

Curiosity has always taken me further than a map.

I'm Romain, a French photographer. Curiosity has taken me across different countries and cultures, and down countless unfamiliar streets.

Photography is my way of slowing down. Of stopping, looking around, and simply being there. I find myself drawn to the small details, the changing light, and the fleeting moments that are so easy to miss.

Through travel, architecture, and everyday life, I try to capture a little of what makes each place special. Not just how it looks, but how it feels to be there.
